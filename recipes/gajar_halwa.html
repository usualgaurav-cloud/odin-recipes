this hot food called gajar ka halwa

    Ingredients
  • gajar AKA carrot
  • almonds
  • milk
  • kaaju
  • ghee
    • Steps
    1. first, peel the carrots and collect them
    2. Now, bring them onto the gas
    3. put milk to make it wet and tasty
    4. also add some almonds
    5. Now, let them cook
